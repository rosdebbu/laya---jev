# KisanZess UI/UX Design System Specification

## 🎨 Aesthetic & Layout Philosophy

The **KisanZess** user interface is built on an **Obsidian & Emerald** enterprise design system crafted for high visual clarity, seamless usability, and 60 FPS hardware-accelerated rendering.

---

## 🏛️ Layout Architecture

### 1. Top Header & APMC Mini-Ticker
- **Brand Group**: Minimalist glowing seed badge (`🌾`) and high-contrast typography.
- **Live Mandi Ticker**: Low-profile horizontal ticker highlighting active wholesale modal rates across key Indian APMC markets (Indore, Agartala, Kolar).
- **Control Group**: Sub-35ms Reflex speed indicator, Vernacular Audio wave visualizer, Sunlight Field Mode button, and one-click Benchmark runner.

### 2. Segmented Navigation Tabs
- `💬 Krishi Varta`: Dual-Brain vernacular chat console and real-time telemetry.
- `🌱 Soil & Crop ML`: Scikit-Learn empirical models and Leaflet.js satellite Earth observation map.
- `🏛️ Krishi Panchayat`: 4-agent deliberative consensus war room for complex farm dilemmas.
- `📜 Khet-Vault`: Auditable persistent farm memory (`FARM_MEMORY.md`).

### 3. Crisis Action Ribbon
Positioned directly above the chat stream for rapid, single-tap assistance during common rural emergencies:
1. `🚨 ₹2,800 दवा vs जैविक ₹180 बचत` — Toxic chemical markup protection.
2. `📉 मंडी आढ़ती हेराफेरी से बचाव (+₹550)` — Wholesale price arbitrage.
3. `🧪 यूरिया छिड़काव vs संतुलित NPK` — Fertilizer overdose prevention.
4. `📸 पत्ती रोग त्वरित Gemini विजन` — Multimodal vision diagnosis.

### 4. Telemetry & ROI Dashboard
- **Kisan Wealth Meter**: Quantifies direct input cost savings (`₹3,820/acre`) and arbitrage gains (`+₹550`).
- **4-Card KPI Grid**: Reflex speedup (68.4x), Cost reduction (97.3%), System 1 ratio (75%), and Memory vault integrity (100%).
- **Latency Benchmark Bars**: Visualizes execution speed (33ms Reflex vs. 2,840ms LLM).
- **Live Decision Trace**: Real-time event log tracking System 1 evaluations.

## ☀️ Outdoor Field Mode
High-contrast daylight theme designed for farmers operating smartphones under direct sunlight, featuring deep contrast ratios and clean, legible typography.
